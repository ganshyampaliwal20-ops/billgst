# Professional Guide: How to Write Blogs for Organic Traffic

To get clear organic traffic from Google, you need to write content that answers what people are searching for.

## 1. Keyword Strategy (Keywords)
Focus on "Long-tail keywords" (Specific phrases).
- **Don't target:** "Billing" (Too broad)
- **Target:** "Free GST billing software for mobile", "Best invoice app for small shop in India"

## 2. Structure of Your Blog
Every blog post should follow this structure for best SEO:

### **H1 Title (Main Heading)**
Must include your main keyword.
*Example: "Top 5 Benefits of Using Free GST Billing Software for Small Business"*

### **Introduction (First 100 words)**
Explain the problem and how your tool solves it.
*Example: "Are you tired of making handwritten bills? Learn how digital billing can save you time."*

### **Subheadings (H2, H3)**
Break down content into readable chunks.
- H2: Why you need digital invoices
- H2: How to save tax with GST reports
- H2: Managing stock easily

### **Content Body**
- Write simple, easy-to-read sentences.
- Use bullet points.
- **Internal Linking**: Always link back to your dashboard or other feature pages.
  *Example: [Create your first free invoice now](/dashboard)*

## 3. Technical SEO Tips
- **Meta Title**: Keep it under 60 characters.
- **Meta Description**: A compelling summary (150-160 chars) that appears in Google.
- **Alt Text for Images**: Describe your images (e.g., "Dashboard screenshot of BillGST showing invoice creation").

## 4. Topics to Write About (Ideas)
1. "How to calculate GST correctly for retailers"
2. "Manual vs Digital Billing: Which is better?"
3. "How to track customer udhaar (credit) on mobile"
4. "Best inventory management tips for grocery stores"

By regularly posting such content on your `/blog` page, Google will start ranking your website higher for these terms.
